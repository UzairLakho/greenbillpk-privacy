Bill references and saved details

The app requires no account. References and fetched bill details are stored in the app's local database. The app does not operate a server that receives or stores these records. Its database and preferences are excluded from Android app backup and device transfer.

Official provider portals

Reference or account numbers are sent to the selected utility's official portal when looking up a bill. Interactive portal pages may set cookies or browser storage, require verification, and handle information under the provider's own policies. Portal pages are displayed using Android WebView. Opening a portal in your browser uses that browser's storage and settings.

Scanning, sharing and printing

Barcode and QR scanning uses Google Code Scanner through Google Play services. The app receives the scanned text to find a reference number. The app does not request camera permission or save camera images. Copy, Share and Print actions disclose the selected number, bill details or link to the destination you choose; printing or saving a PDF is handled by Android and your selected print service.

Advertising

The app includes Google AdMob and Google's User Messaging Platform. When ads are configured, these services may process advertising and device identifiers, IP address, app interactions and diagnostics for advertising, measurement and fraud prevention under Google's advertising policies. The app gates banner requests on UMP's permission to request ads and offers privacy choices when required.

Reminders

Optional background checks send saved PITC references to official bill portals. Android notification permission is required where applicable. You can disable reminders in the app or notifications in Android settings. Gas and K-Electric references require manual checks.

Deletion

Delete saved references from the Saved screen. Clear the app's storage in Android settings to remove the app database, preferences and WebView data, or uninstall the app. Data you explicitly share, print or save elsewhere is managed by its destination. For questions about the app, contact [YOUR_EMAIL]. Third-party provider and advertising data is managed under those services' policies.

Changes

Updates will be posted at this policy's published URL with a revised date.
